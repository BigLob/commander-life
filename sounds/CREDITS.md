## Added in v1.8 (happy and sad)

Short clips (in `sounds/`): victory and level-up stings, win jingles, cheers, game-over and fail
trumpets, from these CC0 pages. Long clips (5 to 12 s, in `sounds/long/`): victory, level-clear and
game-over tunes and sad songs from the same kind of pages, plus full-length cuts of earlier sources
(`laughing-fit`, `fart-symphony`, `accordion-long`, `grunts`, `hungry-ghoul`, `maniacal-laughter`,
`goblin-feast`, `messy-feast`, `ripping-meat`, `troll`, `high-ghost`, `zombie-horde`, `creepy-chant`,
`demon-no-going-back`, `evil-cackle`, `horror-scream`, `vomit`, `diarrhea`, `dog-barking`, `fish-feast`,
credited above).
