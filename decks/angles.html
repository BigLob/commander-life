Where people take our precons

9 of our decks (all but Star Trek and Final Fantasy) · read Nov 16, 2026

How to read it. For each deck: the directions upgraders take it, what they cut, and where they disagree, from EDHREC's numbers plus upgrade videos and articles (sources at the end of each deck).
  • +40% after a card: the share of EDHREC decks built from this box that add it. −80%: the share that cut it. No number means it's outside EDHREC's top 50 adds or top 20 cuts.
  • GC is a Game Changer (counts toward the bracket limit). Any card that isn't Commander-legal gets a red chip; every card here is legal.
  • "Pushed by" and "Cut by" name the sources that do it. Theme counts are how many EDHREC decks for the commander carry that tag.
  • Video content comes from the creators' own decklists and companion articles; YouTube transcripts can't be read from here.

Our newer precons

Silverquill Influence Killian, Decisive Mentor · W/B

Killian goads a creature whenever an enchantment of yours enters and draws when a creature you enchanted attacks. The box is half Auras, half politics ("don't attack me", gifts to opponents, Inklings). Almost every upgrader keeps the Auras and drops the gifts and Inklings; they split on whether to keep the defensive politics.

EDHREC: 6,842 decks built from this box with Killian. Themes those decks are tagged with: Auras 1,314 · Enchantress 1,113 · Forced combat 830 · Voltron 104 · Pillow fort 99 · Politics 115. Most added › Most cut ›

Directions people take it

All-in Auras ("Aura storm")

Cast several cheap Auras a turn: cantrip Auras keep the hand full, cost reducers and enchantress creatures turn each one into a card, and Killian goads with every one. The most common direction by far.

Mesa Enchantress+46%Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice+42%Danitha Capashen, Paragon+30%Feather of Flight+14%Angelic Gift+10%Rune of MortalityUnquestioned Authority+10%ScourgemarkHero of Iroas+9%Sigarda's Aid+13%Retether+25%Tempest Technique+16%Hall of Heliod's Generosity+23%

MTGGoldfish calls the one-mana draw Auras the "secret sauce". EDHREC's Auras theme (the biggest) plays Mesa Enchantress and Hall of Heliod's Generosity more than average.

Pushed by: MTGGoldfish · Flipside Gaming · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHREC upgrade guide · Command Zone #738

One huge evasive attacker (Voltron)

Stack the scaling Auras on one flyer or on Killian and win through commander-style damage. Shares most cards with the Aura plan; the difference is the payoffs.

Ethereal Armor+55%All That Glitters+52%Mantle of the Ancients+29%Twinblade Blessing+8%Brilliant WingsOpen the Armory+13%Duelist's Heritage+12%Boon of the Spirit RealmRashel, Fist of TormSuper State+9%

Ethereal Armor and All That Glitters are the #1 and #2 adds overall. EDHREC's Voltron decks add Duelist's Heritage and Open the Armory about twice as often as the rest.

Pushed by: MTGGoldfish · EDHREC upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · Flipside Gaming

Auras on their creatures (goad and removal)

Put the Auras on opponents' creatures: they goad, draw you cards when those creatures attack, and drain or lock them down. Removal moves from instants to Auras so it also triggers Killian.

Eye of Nidhogg+51%Visions of Brutality+17%Spirit Loop+11%Grievous Wound+9%Minimus Containment+9%Reprobation+9%Spectral GraspNowhere to Run+10%

Eye of Nidhogg is the #3 add (half of all decks) and in the Command Zone's 10. EDHREC's Forced Combat theme plays it in 73%.

Pushed by: EDHREC upgrade guide · MTGGoldfish · Command Zone #738 · Draftsim upgrade guide

Keep the politics as a fort (the Command Zone's take)

Keep Ghostly Prison and the "don't attack me" pieces and add more, so opponents swing at each other while you build. Phase two is one big swing once they're low.

Sphere of Safety+33%Grasp of Fate+9%Dawn's TruceUmbra Mystic+10%Restoration MagicSpellbook Vendor+23%Ondu Spiritdancer+33%Norn's DecreeTenuous TruceTrespasser's Curse

Sphere of Safety is added by a third of decks. EDHREC's small Pillow Fort theme (about 100 decks) adds the Vow cycle and Windborn Muse.

Pushed by: Command Zone #738 · EDHLAB

Enchantment tokens

Enchantment creature tokens that grow with your enchantment count, so each Aura also builds a board.

Sigil of the Empty Throne+29%Hallowed Haunting+12%Daxos the ReturnedGhostly Dancers+13%Soaring Lightbringer+12%Anointed Procession

Sigil of the Empty Throne is added by 29% of decks.

Pushed by: MTGGoldfish · Flipside Gaming · EDHREC upgrade guide · EDHLAB

What people stay away from

Intermediate Chirography−80%Combat Calligrapher−69%Forum Filibuster−38%

Killian doesn't care about tokens, and the Inklings are small. Every guide that names cuts drops these.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · MTGGoldfish · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · Flipside Gaming · Command Zone #738

Gifts that help opponents

Secret Rendezvous−80%Keen Duelist−78%Firemane Commando−62%Mangara, the Diplomat−53%Breena, the Demagogue−33%

They hand cards to the table without adding an Aura. Secret Rendezvous and Keen Duelist are cut by about 4 in 5 decks.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · MTGGoldfish · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · Flipside Gaming · Command Zone #738

Weak deterrents

Tomik, Wielder of Law−73%Nils, Discipline Enforcer−64%

Opponents just pay or attack with one creature; once you have real "don't attack me" effects they add nothing.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · MTGGoldfish · EDHLAB · Command Zone #738

Combat-only and slow cards

Armored Skyhunter−33%Defacing Duskmage−50%Songbirds' BlessingDoomwake Giant−33%Eldrazi Conscription−43%

Once-per-attack triggers and seven-drops can't keep up with several Auras a turn.

Cut by: MTGGoldfish · EDHLAB · Flipside Gaming · Command Zone #738

Sacrifice fodder

Fallen Ideal−56%Screams from Within−36%

The deck isn't aristocrats, and Screams is dead without small creatures to kill.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · MTGGoldfish · EDHLAB · Command Zone #738

Mild recursive Auras

Raffine's Guidance−54%Sentinel's Eyes−45%

Cheap and they come back, but too weak on their own once there are better Auras.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · MTGGoldfish · EDHLAB · Flipside Gaming

Where the sources disagree

  • Politics: MTGGoldfish and Flipside cut Ghostly Prison and every politics card; the Command Zone keeps Ghostly Prison, Mangara and Breena and adds Sphere of Safety and Grasp of Fate. On EDHREC, 74% of decks still play Ghostly Prison.
  • Removal: MTGGoldfish and EDHREC swap the instants (Vanishing Verse, Anguished Unmaking) for Aura removal; the Command Zone adds Path to Exile, and Swords to Plowshares is a top-15 add.
  • Kaya's Ghostform: MTGGoldfish adds it as protection, Draftsim leaves it out ("it reanimates instead of protecting"); 29% of decks add it.
  • Goad does nothing once it's down to two players (EDHLAB), so the Aura-on-their-creatures plan needs a closer.

Alternate commanders on EDHREC: Eriette of the Charmed Apple (653 decks) is played from this box more than the printed alternate, Scriv (503). Scriv decks lean into blink (Ephemerate, Teleportation Circle, Skybind).

Lorehold Spirit Quintorius, History Chaser · R/W

Quintorius makes a 3/2 Spirit whenever cards leave your graveyard, and his loyalty ability discards to draw. Upgraders agree the engine is "put cards in the graveyard, then make them leave", and that discard is the cleanest way in. They pull out the Spirit cards that are only there for the creature type and the death-trigger dragons.

EDHREC: 6,525 decks built from this box with Quintorius. Themes those decks are tagged with: Reanimator 771 · Tokens 727 · Graveyard 709 · Self-mill 408 · Discard 329 · Flashback 300 · Spirits 300. Most added › Most cut ›

Directions people take it

Discard engine

Rummage and loot every turn: each discard fills the graveyard for Quintorius and the payoffs fire on top. MTGGoldfish calls it "the best area to expand".

Bag of Holding+37%Banon, the Returners' Leader+38%Joshua, Phoenix's Dominant+32%Tersa Lightshatter+21%Monument to Endurance+26%Inti, Seneschal of the Sun+10%Hardened Academic+40%Cool but Rude+11%Brass's Tunnel-Grinder+13%Thrilling Discovery+17%Cathartic PyreSeasoned Pyromancer

Banon, Bag of Holding, Joshua and Hardened Academic are all top-7 adds. EDHREC's Discard theme plays Monument to Endurance, Cool but Rude and Inti far more than average.

Pushed by: MTGGoldfish · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHREC upgrade guide · Command Zone #735 · Card Kingdom

Graveyard as a second library

Cards that exile or replay from the graveyard on their own, so Quintorius triggers on every turn, not just yours.

Ark of Hunger+62%Ghost Vacuum+39%Pursue the Past+29%Restoration Seminar+24%Antiquities on the Loose+20%Summoner's Sending+14%Garrison Excavator+20%Crucible of Worlds+11%Idol of Oblivion+10%

Ark of Hunger is the #1 add (62%). EDHREC's Graveyard and Self-mill themes add Restoration Seminar and Pursue the Past most.

Pushed by: EDHREC upgrade guide · Card Kingdom · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · MTGGoldfish

Go wide with Spirits

Make lots of 3/2s, pump them and alpha strike. Token doublers are Card Kingdom's top big-budget priority.

Intangible Virtue+14%On Wings of Gold+36%Drogskol Reinforcements+19%Renewed Solidarity+14%Neyali, Suns' VanguardCaretaker's Talent+17%Professional Face-BreakerBond of DisciplineAkroma's Will+9%Anointed ProcessionMondrak, Glory DominusElspeth, Storm Slayer+9%

EDHREC's Tokens theme adds On Wings of Gold, Renewed Solidarity and Caretaker's Talent more than average.

Pushed by: EDHREC upgrade guide · Card Kingdom · Draftsim upgrade guide

Reanimate and flicker (value creatures)

Bring back creatures with enter-the-battlefield effects and blink them. This is also how the alternate commander, Excava, is built.

Angel of IndemnitySerra ParagonLorehold ArchivistLoran of the Third PathTeleportation CircleEerie InterludeCourt of Ardenvale+7%Loyal Retainers

Only EDHLAB builds mostly this way; most of these cards are already in the box. Card Kingdom says $50 isn't enough for a good Excava flicker deck.

Pushed by: EDHLAB · Command Zone #735

Faster start

Cast Quintorius on turn 3, not 4: two-mana ramp, more cheap permanents, fewer pricey instants and sorceries, and cycling lands so you can play more lands without flooding.

Talisman of ConvictionBoros SignetFellwar StoneOrnithopter of ParadiseKnight of the White OrchidLiquimetal TorqueShenanigans+21%Sundown Pass+32%Sacred Foundry+24%

Sundown Pass (32%) and Sacred Foundry (24%) are the most added lands.

Pushed by: MTGGoldfish · Card Kingdom

What people stay away from

The death-trigger dragons

Ao, the Dawn Sky−69%Atsushi, the Blazing Sky−66%

Their value comes when they die, and discard sends cards to the graveyard without dying. Cut by about two thirds of decks.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · Command Zone #735

A sweeper that kills your own team

Wave of Reckoning−75%

Your board is mostly 3/2 Spirits. Cut by 75% of decks and every guide.

Cut by: MTGGoldfish · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · Command Zone #735

Spirits only for the creature type

Drumbellower−49%Balefire Liege−30%Kami of Ancient Law−57%Remorseful Cleric−42%Spirit of ResilienceVenerable Warsinger

They do little beyond being Spirits; Quintorius's −4 already gives the team vigilance.

Cut by: MTGGoldfish · EDHREC upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · Command Zone #735

Cards that feed opponents

Secret Rendezvous−81%Naktamun Lorespinner−65%

Three cards to an opponent, or a Wheel of Fortune that refills everyone. Secret Rendezvous is the #1 cut (81%).

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB · Command Zone #735

Narrow or taxing cards

White Orchid Phantom−59%Monologue Tax−45%Bitterthorn, Nissa's Animus−57%Claim Jumper−42%Lorehold CharmFateful Tempest−63%

Land destruction and a tax effect don't advance the plan; Bitterthorn and Claim Jumper are slow ramp.

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB

Spells-matter and big flashback

Lorehold, the HistorianAdvanced Reconstruction

Only about 12 instants and sorceries; heavy flashback empties the graveyard Quintorius needs.

Cut by: Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHLAB

Where the sources disagree

  • Bag of Holding: a top-5 add and in MTGGoldfish's and EDHREC's lists, but Draftsim leaves it out because its graveyard exile is mandatory and can strand your own recursion.
  • Flashback: EDHREC's Flashback theme (300 decks) adds Pursue the Past and the Flashback card; Draftsim and EDHLAB warn it eats the graveyard.
  • The Command Zone also cuts Moonshaker Cavalry and Squee from the box; most others keep both.

Excava, the Risen Past is barely played (101 decks vs 6,525 for Quintorius). EDHREC's guide cuts it, and Card Kingdom calls Quintorius "without a doubt the better option".

Living Energy Saheeli, Radiant Creator · U/R/G

Saheeli gives energy for each artifact or Artificer spell and, each combat, pays three energy for a 5/5 hasty copy of one of your permanents. Upgraders split three ways: more energy and energy finishers, bigger things to copy, or more artifact tokens. The Thopter cards, the legendary copy targets and the slow mana sinks are what they drop.

EDHREC: 4,215 decks built from this box with Saheeli. Themes those decks are tagged with: Energy 1,321 · Artifacts 828 · Clones 256 · Tokens 327 · Artificers 144 · Vehicles 34. Most added › Most cut ›

Directions people take it

Big energy (more energy, energy finishers)

Double the energy you make and spend it to win: damage equal to energy gained, energy-fed threats, counters paid with energy.

Izzet Generatorium+67%Aether Refinery+54%Gonti's Aether Heart+48%Roil Cartographer+54%Aether Revolt+38%Electrosiphon+43%Amped Raptor+34%Volatile Stormdrake+33%Galvanic Discharge+27%Aether Spike+23%Primal Prayers+18%Stone Idol Generator+19%HELIOS One+22%

The top 6 adds are all energy cards (Izzet Generatorium 67%). Energy is EDHREC's biggest theme for Saheeli (1,321 decks). The Command Zone's 10 are nearly all energy.

Pushed by: Command Zone #653 · Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHREC upgrade guide · Card Kingdom · Gathering Games upgrade guide

Bigger copy targets

Saheeli's copy is a 5/5 haste that dies at end of turn, so pick things whose value is on entering or attacking, or that make more bodies. Gathering Games' combo: Threefold Thunderhulk copied by Saheeli and Brudiclad, about nineteen tokens in one swing.

Threefold Thunderhulk+9%Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer+30%Blaster Hulk+24%The Motherlode, Excavator+24%Octomancer+16%Aurora Shifter+23%Behemoth of Vault 0+22%Robobrain War MindGarruk's Uprising+17%Esoteric Duplicator+9%Saheeli, the Sun's Brilliance+14%Hoarding Dragon

EDHREC's Clones theme plays Garruk's Uprising, Octomancer, Esoteric Duplicator and Threefold Thunderhulk about twice as often as other Saheeli decks.

Pushed by: Gathering Games upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · Command Zone #653 · EDHREC upgrade guide

Artifact tokens and Artificers

Make and double artifact tokens, so Saheeli's energy flows and Artificer payoffs grow. EDHREC's non-budget list builds this way.

EDHREC's Artifacts theme (828 decks) and Artificers theme add Jhoira, Padeem and Oviya, Automech Artisan.

Pushed by: EDHREC upgrade guide

Blue value and big ETBs (Card Kingdom's take)

Bootleggers' Stash is the #1 cut (70%). Gathering Games: if you're using Retrofitter Foundry, "something has gone wrong".

Cut by: EDHREC upgrade guide · Gathering Games upgrade guide · Draftsim upgrade guide · Command Zone #653 · Card Kingdom

The planeswalker Saheeli

Saheeli, Sublime Artificer−43%

The deck doesn't make enough tokens for it.

Cut by: Draftsim upgrade guide · EDHREC upgrade guide

Where the sources disagree

  • Counterspells: EDHREC's budget list and the Command Zone cut Disallow (and EDHREC Arcane Denial), but Electrosiphon and Tune the Narrative are top-10 adds, so the counters change rather than vanish.
  • Thopters: EDHREC's small Artificers theme still plays Sai in 64% of decks, so the artifact-token angle keeps what the energy angle cuts.
  • Ramp: Explosive Vegetation is cut by 44% of decks, while Cards Realm adds Cultivate and Kodama's Reach.

Pia Nalaar, Chief Mechanic (the box's alternate, 305 decks) is the vehicles build; Saheeli has 4,215.

Eternal Might Temmet, Naktamun's Will · W/U/B

Temmet loots when attacking and pumps your Zombies when you draw. The box mixes Zombies with non-Zombie embalm and eternalize cards and a discard theme for the alternate commander. Upgraders make it a real Zombie deck: lords, mass recursion and bursts of card draw. The non-Zombie embalm creatures are the most-cut cards of any of our decks.

EDHREC: 6,447 decks built from this box with Temmet. Themes those decks are tagged with: Zombies 1,016 · Tokens 541 · Card draw 328 · Reanimator 291 · Discard 278 · Wheels 65 · Aristocrats 64. Most added › Most cut ›

Directions people take it

Pure Zombie typal

Lords and anthems, Zombies that make more Zombies, and mass recursion to rebuild after a wipe.

Varina, Lich Queen+51%Zul Ashur, Lich Lord+47%Diregraf Colossus+38%Death Baron+36%Diregraf Captain+33%Lord of the Undead+11%Undead Warchief+15%Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver+23%Necroduality+42%Zombie Apocalypse+29%Endless Ranks of the Dead+27%Liliana, Dreadhorde General+24%Living Death+6%

Varina (51%), Zul Ashur (47%), Necroduality and Diregraf Colossus are top-5 adds and all in the Command Zone's 10. Zombies is EDHREC's biggest Temmet theme (1,016 decks).

Pushed by: Command Zone #654 · Farwater MTG · CoolStuffInc · Card Kingdom · Draftsim

Draw bursts to pump the team

Temmet pumps on draws, so draw several cards right before combat and swing.

Kindred Discovery+31%Windfall+20%Titan of LittjaraTeferi's Ageless Insight+13%Distant Melody+18%Read the RunesGraveborn Muse+9%God-Eternal BontuBrainstorm+27%Stinging Study

Pushed by: Card Kingdom · Draftsim · CoolStuffInc · Command Zone #654

Gravecrawler loops (Bracket 3)

Rooftop Storm makes Zombies free; with Gravecrawler and a sacrifice outlet it loops for drain and cards. The Command Zone lists it as a separate "combo upgrade".

Rooftop Storm+58%Warren Soultrader+10%Carrion FeederPhyrexian AltarAshnod's AltarGrimgrin, Corpse-Born+7%Bolas's CitadelGC

Rooftop Storm is the #1 add (58%). EDHREC's Bracketeering article uses this package to move the deck from Bracket 2 to 3, so it's a power jump for our table.

Pushed by: Command Zone #654 · Card Kingdom · EDHREC

Discard and loot

Lean into Temmet's loot: make Zombies or value whenever you discard. Draftsim builds this way; CoolStuffInc says discard only suits the alternate commander, Hashaton.

EDHREC's Discard theme (278 decks) plays Monument to Endurance and Cryptcaller Chariot well above average.

Blue interaction

Counterspell is added by 18% of decks.

Never // Return−62%Gate to the Afterlife−64%God-Pharaoh's Gift−65%Commence the Endgame−61%Dread Summons−34%Cast Out−55%

Cut by: Command Zone #654 · Farwater MTG · CoolStuffInc · Draftsim

A wipe your own board survives poorly

Dusk // Dawn−43%

Temmet's pumped team is usually too big for Dusk to be one-sided.

Cut by: Farwater MTG · CoolStuffInc · Draftsim

Tapped Deserts and mana rocks

Desert of the True−36%Desert of the Mindful−36%Desert of the Glorified−32%Commander's Sphere−32%

They enter tapped and the deck has few desert payoffs; Card Kingdom would rather cast a turn-3 Zombie than a rock.

Cut by: EDHREC · Card Kingdom · Draftsim · CoolStuffInc

Where the sources disagree

  • Sacrifice: Draftsim drops the death and sacrifice cards (Corpse Augur, Fleshbag Marauder, Priest of the Crossing); the combo builds add sacrifice outlets instead.
  • Discard: Draftsim leans into it, CoolStuffInc cuts it (Archfiend of Ifnir, Twisted Abomination) as Hashaton's theme.
  • Land count: Draftsim and CoolStuffInc go down to 37 lands; Card Kingdom stays at 38 or more because of the constant discard.

Hashaton, Scarab's Fist (1,523 decks from this box) is a different, stronger deck: discard and reanimator with cEDH-style staples (Esper Sentinel, Rhystic Study, Psychic Frog). Card Kingdom says it isn't a budget build. Varina (690) is also played as the commander.

First Flight Isperia, Supreme Judge · W/U

Isperia draws you a card whenever a creature attacks you, and the box is flyers plus Sphinxes plus cards that want to be attacked. Upgraders mostly turn it into a lean flyers deck and often change the commander: on EDHREC more decks from this box run Kangee, Sky Warden than Isperia.

Flyers with anthems and cost reducers
