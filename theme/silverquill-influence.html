How these were picked
  • The theme is written as card tests from the direction and the commander's rules text. A card has to pass one to be picked on its own merits.
  • "Built this way" counts the 1055 of 3065 Archidekt decks with the commander that have the most on-theme cards (the top third).
  • Rank: the mean of the two shares, plus 3 points for each written guide that adds the card. Power and price don't count.
  • Off-theme cards (removal, ramp, staples) get in only if decks built this way play them as much as the 15th on-theme pick and at least two guides add them.
  • Up to 3 Game Changers in the whole deck. Commander Spellbook checks the finished deck; a card that makes an early two-card infinite, mass land denial or extra turns is dropped.
  • Cuts: box spells that aren't on theme, ranked by how often decks built this way leave them out and EDHREC's cut share.
  • Lands: kept out of the 15 for 15. The land version swaps 10 basics for 10 nonbasic lands, on-theme lands (those passing a theme test that decks built this way or a source use) first, then the most played.
