How these were picked

Bar height (29 to 31" seat), an upholstered seat and a back that rises at least about 9" above it, chair-shaped rather than stool-shaped, and a mid-century look. Prices are each store's own listing on Nov 10, 2026, per chair.

Comfort is my rating out of 5 from the specs: cushion, back height and shape, arms. Wipe-clean means leather or vegan leather; "Stain-resistant" is performance fabric, which spot-cleans.

Mid tier is up to $720 a chair; "Step up" ones run $749 to $900. Wayfair, West Elm, Pottery Barn and Crate & Barrel block automated lookups, so they're not here.
