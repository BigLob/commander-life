Where people take our precons

10 of our decks (all but Star Trek) plus Squirreled Away (we run Chatterfang) · read

How to read it. For each deck: the directions upgraders take it, what they cut, and where they disagree, from EDHREC's numbers plus upgrade videos and written guides (sources at the end of each deck).
  • +40% after a card: the share of EDHREC decks built from this box that add it. −80%: the share that cut it. No number means it's outside EDHREC's top 50 adds or top 20 cuts.
  • Revival Trance has two sections, one per commander, because Terra and Celes decks are built differently. In the Celes section a card's 48% is the share of all EDHREC Celes decks that play it, and cut shares are left out (the box's cut numbers come mostly from Terra decks).
  • Our pick (outlined) is the direction we chose for the deck, with 15 cards to add, most-backed first. Under each card is how many guides and videos add it (tap the image to see which); "EDHREC only" means no guide names it but EDHREC's add share does. The other directions are folded underneath.
  • A red box means most decks or many sources change the commander; it lists the commanders they use and EDHREC's split.
  • Tap a card image to enlarge it; tap its name to open it on Scryfall. The switch below turns the images off if you want the short list back.
  • GC is a Game Changer (counts toward the bracket limit). Any card that isn't Commander-legal gets a red chip; every card here is legal.
  • "Pushed by" and "Cut by" name the sources that do it. Theme counts are how many EDHREC decks for the commander carry that tag.
  • Videos were read from their YouTube transcripts, with the creators' own Moxfield or Archidekt lists for exact cuts. From play lines report how a direction went for someone who played it: a creator's playtest or stream, or a player's deck a creator reviewed.
