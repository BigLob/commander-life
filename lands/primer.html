Nonbasic lands: primer

1,227 Commander-legal nonbasic lands in 40 categories · popularity = EDHREC rank · price = cheapest paper printing (Scryfall, Nov 2026)

Bottom line

  • The job: make every color on time. A land that enters tapped costs a turn of mana; untapped duals are the single biggest mana-base upgrade.
  • Auto-includes: Command Tower (#2 card on EDHREC), Exotic Orchard, Path of Ancestry, Reliquary Tower, Rogue's Passage. All under $3.
  • Best value per dollar: check lands and pain lands ($0.25 to $0.50) and Bond lands (~$5, untapped every game in multiplayer). Shocks (~$5) are the first "real" upgrade.
  • Luxury tier: fetches ($11 to $36), surveil lands and triomes (~$12 to $17), channel lands ($6 to $46). Original duals ($220 to $400) aren't worth it at our table.
  • Our precons: 143 nonbasics across 13 decks, about half enter tapped. Each deck can fix most of that for $1 to $5 (table at the bottom).
  • Utility lands are free spells in a land slot: graveyard hate (Bojuka Bog), unblockable (Rogue's Passage), removal that is also a land (Boseiju, Otawara). Room for 3 to 8 depending on color count.

Building a mana base

  • Count: 36 to 38 lands plus about 10 ramp pieces. MDFC spell lands count as half a land.
  • Duals by colors: 2 colors, 6 to 10 duals; 3 colors, 12 to 18; 4 to 5 colors, as many as fit. Fewer colors leaves room for utility lands.
  • Tapped-land budget: 4 or fewer. Cut temples, gain lands and tapped tri-lands first.
  • Build around basic types: shocks, triomes, surveil, battle and typed tapped duals count as Plains, Island and so on, so fetches find them and check lands and snarls see them.
  • Keep some basics: Myriad Landscape, battle lands and opponents' Path to Exile need them; Blood Moon punishes greedy bases.
  • Landfall and lands decks: fetches and bounce lands double triggers; add Field of the Dead and land recursion.
  • Upgrade order: check + pain lands → Bond lands → shocks → fetches → surveil lands / triomes.
