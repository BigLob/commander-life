# Buying guide: what a fair price is

Reference for shopping at the local game store. Prices are US dollars before tax, collected
Oct 26, 2026. MSRP is Wizards' suggested retail price. Store prices drift, so re-check any number
here that is more than a few months old.

How to judge a price: **Good** = buy it. **OK** = normal store price. **High** = buy elsewhere or wait.

For upgrading decks, buying single cards beats buying packs.

## Xenagos deck: buy the cheap cards, proxy the rest

Our Xenagos, the Party Hugger deck (the card page at `xenagos.html`) is 100 cards: 34 lands and 66 other
cards. Prices are the ones on that page (Oct 2026). The plan is real copies of the cheap cards and proxies
of the expensive ones.

- **Lands are left out of both lists.** Basics are $0.09 and the other 16 lands cost $2.03 or less, so there is
  nothing to save by proxying them.
- **Buy** the 50 cards that cost $3.63 or less, plus Recycle, Howling Mine, Magus of the Vineyard and
  Shizuko, Caller of Autumn (each $4.06 to $4.92, bought on purpose): **$64.86**.
- **Proxy** the other 16, which would cost $164.30 real. That is about 76% real and 24% proxied by card count.
- A straight 80/20 split of all 100 cards (cheapest 80 real) would be $53.45 real. Going to 85 real cards
  raises that to $76.99 and 90 to $107.07.
- The proxy sheet is 2 pages (16 cards, 2.5 x 3.5 in, 3 x 3 per Letter page) with the regular printing of each card.
