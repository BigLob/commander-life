# Upgrade guide: the five Starter Commander Decks (2022)

Plan: swap about 15 cards in each deck, picked from 30 suggestions per deck (lands included), at three power levels.
The final goal is Bracket 3 with all five decks level: see "Bracket 3, all five decks level" below.
Researched Nov 4, 2026.

## How this was counted

- **Written guides (13):** EDHREC's Scrap Trawlers upgrade guide for all five decks, Cards Realm's "Upgrading Commander
  Precon" for First Flight, Grave Danger, Chaos Incarnate and Token Triumph, and cardgamebase's upgrade guide for First
  Flight, Grave Danger, Draconic Destruction and Token Triumph. A guide counts a card if it names it as an addition,
  including its budget and premium picks.
- **Archidekt decklists (1,617):** every public Commander deck on Archidekt with the precon's commander (the 300 most
  viewed, plus every deck named after the precon or with "precon", "upgrade" or "starter" in its name), 6,500 in all. A
  deck is kept only if it still has at least 30 of the precon's nonbasic cards (so it was built from the precon) and adds
  at least 5 new ones. Only one deck per owner is kept, and exact copies are dropped. Kept: First Flight 204, Grave Danger 373, Chaos Incarnate 244,
  Draconic Destruction 452, Token Triumph 344. A decklist counts a card if it plays it.
- **"x of y"**: x lists recommend the card, out of y lists (the guides plus the decklists for that deck). "Guides" shows
  the written guides alone. "EDHREC" is the share of EDHREC's upgraded decks of that precon that added the card (a separate,
  larger sample of 701 to 2,730 decks).
- Each deck's 30 are its top 22 spells and top 8 lands by x of y. Only cards in the deck's colors and legal in Commander
  count. Game Changers (cards that push a deck to Bracket 3) are listed separately under each table.
- Prices: Scryfall (TCGplayer market), Nov 4, 2026.
- Not used: StarCityGames' "Upgrades" series (video only), Vault Games' budget series (the site refused the connection),
  Moxfield and Reddit (they block scripts), and YouTube.
- Decklists show what players built, not what won. A card that 1 in 5 lists play is a common choice, not a proven upgrade.

### First Flight: Isperia, Supreme Judge (W U)
39 lands, 26 creatures. 25 of the 26 creatures fly (all but Angler Turtle).
