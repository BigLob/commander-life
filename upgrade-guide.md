# Upgrade guide: the five Starter Commander Decks (2022)

Plan: swap about 15 cards in each deck, picked from 30 suggestions per deck (lands included), at
three power levels. Counts below are from the decklists in `index.html` (`DECKS`) and card text
from Scryfall, Nov 4, 2026. A card can count toward more than one mechanic.

## What each deck is doing

### First Flight: Isperia, Supreme Judge (W U)
39 lands, 26 creatures. 25 of the 26 creatures fly (all but Angler Turtle).

| Mechanic | Cards | Which |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flying creatures | 25 | Everything but Angler Turtle |
| Flyer payoffs (anthems, cost cuts, draw) | 17 | Archon of Redemption, Aven Gagglemaster, Empyrean Eagle, Favorable Winds, Gravitational Shift, Jubilant Skybonder, Kangee's Lieutenant, Kangee, Rally of Wings, Sephara, Skycat Sovereign, Steel-Plume Marshal, Thunderclap Wyvern, Tide Skimmer, Warden of Evos Isle, Windreader Sphinx, Winged Words |
| Flying token makers | 9 | Emeria Angel, Faerie Formation, Hanged Executioner, Inspired Sphinx, Migratory Route, Moorland Haunt, Sharding Sphinx, Skycat Sovereign, Storm Herd |
| Make them attack you (Isperia draws) | 3 | Angler Turtle, Bident of Thassa, Gideon Jura |
| Punish attackers | 4 | Aetherize, Condemn, Ever-Watching Threshold, Soul Snare |

Isperia's own theme (get attacked, draw) has only 7 cards behind it; the deck is really flyer
aggro. That split is a big reason it's the weakest of the five (18.4% win rate).
